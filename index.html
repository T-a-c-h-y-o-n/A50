AI Act 50 is in force. Most sites we check are not ready.

  • Chatbots and AI assistants must be disclosed to visitors - a widget in the corner is not enough.
  • AI-generated media must be marked when published - provenance metadata alone does not count.
  • Emotion recognition and biometric categorisation require prior notice.
  • Disclosures hidden in comments, metadata or display:none are treated as missing.

The scan checks your live pages against the AI Act 50 transparency obligations and gives you a sourced, evidence-backed gap report.

How the detection actually works

  • We match script domains, global JS objects, iframe sources and API endpoints against a versioned signature database - not a guess, and not a language model.
  • Media provenance is read from C2PA manifests, XMP metadata and PNG text chunks - machine-readable markers only.
  • Every finding repeats the exact string it matched on, so you can verify or dismiss it in seconds.
  • Disclosure detection is multilingual and checks visible text only - hidden markup is treated as missing.
  • Nothing is presented as a legal verdict. A finding tells you what was observed, not what to conclude.

Questions

What exactly does the scan check?

Your live pages against the EU AI Act 50 transparency obligations: conversational AI widgets, AI-generated media with provenance marks, emotion recognition and biometric tools, and whether visitors are told about them. Every finding carries its evidence: page URL, selector, matched string and artifact hash.

How does the detection work?

Deterministic rules: script domains, global JS objects, iframe sources and API endpoints matched against a versioned signature database of 19 known providers. Media provenance is read from C2PA manifests, XMP metadata and PNG text chunks. Disclosure detection is multilingual and checks visible text only.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a sourced checklist, not legal advice. The scan reports what was observed in your pages. It does not tell you whether you are compliant, and it does not replace a legal review.

What if a finding is wrong?

Treat every finding as a review task. Each finding shows the exact string it matched on, so a weak finding can be dismissed in seconds - and your record shows you looked.

What does "NOT_VERIFIABLE" mean?

The scanner could not reach a conclusion: the page was not fetched, JavaScript was not rendered, or no explicit signal exists. NOT_VERIFIABLE is reported with the reason, never guessed.

What does "PARTIAL" mean?

One or more planned pages could not be fetched. The report lists which pages failed and why. A partial scan is never presented as a full scan.

What do I get for €99?

A single-site scan with the one-page PDF report: findings sorted critical to low, each with evidence and remediation, plus the sources and their review dates.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Paste a URL, read a result. Everything is in plain language, and the reason for each finding is written out.

Is my data secure, and who can see it?

Your URL is used to run your scan. It isn't published and isn't sold or shared with third parties. Scan results are stored with a signed token and are only readable with that token.
